About Our Inn

Founded in 1920, our Bed & Breakfast has been a sanctuary for travelers seeking the perfect balance between nature and luxury.

Our Story

It began with a simple idea: to create a place where time slows down. Over the decades, we have hosted thousands of guests, each adding their own story to our history.

Our Philosophy

Rooted in Community

We believe in the power of local. From our soaps to our sausages, everything is sourced from Vermont artisans and farmers.

Stewardship

Our property is 80% solar-powered, and we maintain a zero-waste kitchen policy to protect the forest we call home.
