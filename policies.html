Guest Policies

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in time is 3:00 PM. Check-out time is 11:00 AM.

Cancellation Policy

Full refund for cancellations made within 48 hours of booking. 50% refund for cancellations made at least 7 days before check-in.

Accessibility

Our "Sand Loft" and public dining areas are fully ADA compliant. We offer step-free access from the parking lot and visual fire alarms upon request.

Bringing a Pet?

We welcome well-behaved dogs in our dedicated pet-friendly rooms.

  • $50 per stay cleaning fee applies.
  • Pets must not be left unattended in rooms.
  • Complimentary dog bed and bowls provided.
